# My voice

Copy this file to `voice.md` and replace everything with your own habits. `voice.md` is gitignored.
Describe patterns and use invented examples: no real names, projects, clients or quoted messages.

- **Length:** for example, one line for most messages; two or three short lines for a longer thought.
- **Capitals and punctuation:** for example, sentence case, no full stop at the end of a line.
- **Asks:** for example, "can you ... please?", "when you have a minute, ...".
- **Warmth:** for example, "thanks!", a smiley now and then, or none at all.
- **Words you use often:** for example, "btw", "FYI".
- **Links and tickets:** for example, a bare URL, or `[PROJ-123](url)` followed by the title.
- **Don't:** for example, formal openings or closings, long explanations, bold.

Example of a first message of the day:

```
good morning

can you check the staging plan on [!885](https://gitlab.example.com/group/project/-/merge_requests/885) when you get a minute? no rush
```
